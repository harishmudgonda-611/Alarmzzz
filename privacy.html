Alarmzzz: Snooze Is Dead - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Alarmzzz: Snooze Is Dead ("the app") is an Android alarm clock by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

The app has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect your personal information. Everything it stores stays on your phone.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your alarm time, your streak of completed mornings, and an optional partner name that you type in. This data is kept only in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions the app uses

Alarms and exact alarms: to ring at the time you choose.
Notifications and full-screen notifications: to show the alarm screen when the alarm rings.
Foreground service (media playback): to play the alarm sound until you finish the wake-up challenges.
Physical activity (activity recognition): to count your 10 steps with your phone's step sensor. Step data is used on your phone during the alarm only. It is not saved or sent anywhere.
Run at startup: to set your alarm again after your phone restarts.

Sharing

The "Share with partner" and "Invite a partner" buttons open Android's share menu. The message includes your streak and the partner name you typed. You choose the app and the person to send it to. We do not see or store these messages. The app you choose to share with has its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. It does not show ads, sell data, or share data with third parties.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
